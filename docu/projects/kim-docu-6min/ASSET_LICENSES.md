# ASSET_LICENSES.md — kim-docu-6min

Status of every picture, sound and data source used by the 6-minute documentary.

## 1. Real photographs

**No real photograph is used in this video.** Nothing was downloaded, nothing was approved, nothing is hot-linked.

Reason (checked, not assumed): the build environment reaches the internet only through an egress proxy with an allow-list.
Every image host was refused with *"403 – no rule or network setting allows this host"*:
`commons.wikimedia.org`, `upload.wikimedia.org`, `www.loc.gov`, `www.archives.gov`, `images.unsplash.com`, `www.flickr.com` and `live.staticflickr.com`.
Following the project rule, the restriction was **not** circumvented (no mirror, cache, proxy, VPN or search-engine thumbnail).
A photograph whose licence page cannot be opened and read cannot be approved, so none is approved.

| Planned slot | Place / event | Candidate source class | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-02 | Los Angeles courthouse, 1995 trial | Wikimedia Commons / US government (public domain) | not reachable → **not used** |
| P-04 | Montreal, 1976 Olympics | Wikimedia Commons / Government of Canada | not reachable → **not used** |
| P-05 | Family and TV production, 2007 | Wikimedia Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA only) | not reachable → **not used** |
| P-06 | SKIMS, 2025 | Company press kit / Commons | not reachable, and company photos are not reusable → **not used** |

If the person who owns the project opens `commons.wikimedia.org`, `upload.wikimedia.org` (and optionally `loc.gov`, `archives.gov`) for the workspace,
the slot gate (`photo_slots.json`) accepts a photo only when **all** of these fields are filled: photographer / owner, page URL, licence name + version,
commercial use allowed, modification allowed, attribution text. No field → no photo. Nothing else changes in the video.

## 2. What is on screen instead (never presented as a photograph)

Every picture in the film is one of three honest kinds, and the kind is written on screen in a small badge:

| Badge | What it is | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `CARTOON RECONSTRUCTION` | an illustrated scene drawn for this film (our own characters, our own generic buildings — the courthouse is a generic columned building, not the real one) | own SVG art in `docu/engine/kit` + `shots.js` |
| `DOCUMENTARY GRAPHIC` | dates, names, numbers, charts, case-file / TV-screen / stamp icons on an archive-board background | own SVG art |
| `ANIMATED MAP` | Albers-projection map of the United States with route and pins | own SVG; geometry is simplified coastline drawn by hand in `shots.js` (no map data downloaded) |

Characters are original cartoon designs (not likenesses traced from photographs). No real person's photograph, voice or logo is used.
